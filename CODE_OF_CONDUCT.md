# Code of Conduct

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project — in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions — to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Owning our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what's best for the project and the people using it.

Examples of behaviour that isn't acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — the repository, its issues and pull requests — and when someone is officially representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the project maintainer through GitHub (a direct message to the repository owner). Don't report it in a public issue.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy and safety of the person reporting will be respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow them. Depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is, the response may be:

1. **A private, written warning** explaining what was inappropriate.
2. **A temporary restriction** from interacting with the project.
3. **A permanent ban** from the project.
